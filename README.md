# **ProtoBase 🗄️**

A rapid, zero-setup, offline-first meta-database tool for "vibe coding" and capturing structured data on the fly.

## **Features**

* **Zero Backend Required:** Uses your browser's local storage to save data instantly.  
* **Offline Capable:** Works perfectly even when you lose cell service inside a restaurant.  
* **Dynamic Schema:** Define your JSON keys (properties) on the fly, but automatically remembers your previous structure to maintain consistency.  
* **Export Ready:** One-click export to a raw .json file for immediate use in future AI prompts or application building.

## **How to use**

1. Add your properties (e.g., Name, Vibe, Rating).  
2. Add your details.  
3. Click Save.  
4. At the end of your session, click **Export** to download your database.
